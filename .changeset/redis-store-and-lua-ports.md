Implement pure TypeScript sliding window algorithms (`slidingWindowLog` and `slidingWindowCounter`) in `@reqnx/core`, and deliver `@reqnx/redis` with `RedisStore`, structural adapters for node-redis and ioredis, EVALSHA runner with self-healing EVAL fallback, and exact 64-bit integer Lua ports for all four core algorithms (`fixedWindow`, `tokenBucket`, `slidingWindowLog`, `slidingWindowCounter`).
