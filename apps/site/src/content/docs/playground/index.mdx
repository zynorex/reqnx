## Scenario Presets Explained

- **Basic Burst**: Fires 7 rapid requests against a limit of 5. The first 5 are admitted (`allowed: true`); the 6th and 7th are denied (`allowed: false`, HTTP 429).
- **Window Rollover**: Sends 5 requests to exhaust the window, advances the simulated clock past the 10-second boundary, then fires 3 more. Demonstrates clean window expiration and quota recovery.
- **Boundary Burst**: Sends 5 requests at the end of window 1 ($t = 9.9\text{s}$), then 5 requests immediately at the beginning of window 2 ($t = 10.1\text{s}$). All 10 are admitted across a 200ms interval, demonstrating the $2\times$ boundary burst.
- **Multi-Key Isolation**: Alternates requests between two keys (`alice` and `bob`). Shows that rate limits and window state are strictly isolated per key.
